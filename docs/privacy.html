ClipBot Privacy Policy

ClipBot is a personal tool run by its owner to cut gameplay clips and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With your permission, ClipBot uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and read basic channel statistics for the owner's channel.

What it stores

An access token is stored as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret in the owner's repository and is used only to upload to the owner's channel. Clip titles, schedule times and YouTube video IDs are saved in the repository. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Revoking access

You can remove ClipBot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ClipBot's use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
